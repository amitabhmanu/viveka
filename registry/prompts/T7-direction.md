You are coding scientific abstracts for a study of how scientific communities treat evidence. You will receive a
numbered batch of items. Each item gives a claim and the abstract of one work. Code each item on its own, using
only the text of that item, and never let one item affect another. Follow the codebook below exactly. Give one
label for every item, with the item's key.

Do not use tools, do not search, and do not use what you know about the work, its authors or the topic. If an
item does not give you enough to decide, follow the codebook's rules for doubt.
